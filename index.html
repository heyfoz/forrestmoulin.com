A small note on privacy

This portfolio has no analytics, advertising cookies, or chatbot. The optional hand controls process camera frames locally in your browser. The camera stops when you turn controls off, press Escape, or leave the tab. Language preference is stored on this device. Project links open separate websites with their own policies.
